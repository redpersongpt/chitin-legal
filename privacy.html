Privacy Policy

Chitin (net.buglens.app) · Last updated: July 2026

Chitin identifies insects, spiders, and other arthropods from a photo you choose. This page explains what happens to your data.

What is sent off your device

Every time you identify a photo, that photo is sent to our backend, which uses Google Gemini to analyze it and return a likely species, a confidence level, and a short written explanation. This is the only way Chitin performs an identification — there is no offline or on-device recognition mode. The photo is processed to produce your result and is not retained by us afterward for any other purpose. It is not used to train any model, and it is not linked to your identity — Chitin has no accounts and sends no personal identifiers with the image.

What stays on your device

Your identification history, including thumbnail images, is stored only locally on your device. It is never uploaded to or stored on our servers. You can delete it at any time from Profile → Clear local history. Uninstalling the app removes it entirely.

What we do not collect

  • No account or sign-in, so no personal profile data.
  • No advertising identifier and no App Tracking Transparency prompt — Chitin does not track you across apps or websites.
  • No location, contacts, or precise/coarse location access.
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Purchases

Subscription purchases and restores are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment details, and purchase records are not linked to a personal profile on our side.

Permissions

Camera and Photo Library access are requested only to obtain the photo you want identified. Neither is used for any other purpose.

Third-party processor

Google Gemini, reached through our own backend server, is the only third party involved in producing an identification. Chitin's iOS app never talks to Gemini directly and never holds any provider API keys.

Children

Chitin does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no account system and no user-generated content shared with others.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what data is collected or how it is used, the "Last updated" date above will change and, where required, we will notify you in-app.
